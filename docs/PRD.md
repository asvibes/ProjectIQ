## 1. Problem

Tender packages for industrial projects are long, scattered across PDFs, addenda and bills of quantities, and full of conditions that decide eligibility: certifications, past-project experience, bonding limits, deadlines, liquidated damages. Contractors decide whether to bid under time pressure, often by skimming. Missing a mandatory requirement wastes bid effort; misjudging cash-flow or capacity exposure can lose money on a won contract.

Generic AI chat tools summarise documents but do not tell the reader whether a statement is actually in the source, whether the company can really meet it, or what is still unknown.

## 2. Product vision

ProjectIQ turns a tender and a company profile into an **evidence-backed bid/no-bid briefing**:

1. Every claim about the tender points to a quotation and its location in the source document.
2. Every claim about the company carries a verification status and date.
3. Where evidence is missing, the answer is **Unknown**, not a guess.
4. Money calculations are deterministic and reproducible, not generated by a language model.
5. Documents are processed locally (Gemma through Ollama) so confidential tender material does not leave the machine.
6. People make the decision. The tool prepares it.

## 3. Target users

| User | Needs |
|---|---|
| Bid manager / tender lead (primary) | Fast, trustworthy read of a tender; a clear list of gaps; a defensible briefing |
| Estimator / commercial manager | Cost, pricing, break-even and cash-flow feasibility |
| Director / decision maker | A concise, evidence-backed go / no-go brief with risks and scenarios |
| Technical lead / operations | Which roles and equipment a tender needs versus what is available |

Primary customer profile: small and mid-size industrial construction and engineering contractors that lack a dedicated bid-analysis team.

## 4. Principles

- **Evidence or nothing.** No extracted fact without a quotation. The data model enforces this for extracted facts.
- **Unknown is a valid answer.** Missing data stays `NULL` / `Unknown` and is never silently filled.
- **Human verification.** Extracted values start as *Requires review* and become *Verified* only by a person.
- **Employed is not available.** Workforce headcount and availability are separate fields.
- **Local first.** No cloud dependency for core operation.
- **Decision support, not decision making.** The product never issues an automatic bid decision.

## 5. Core workflow

1. **Set up the company profile**: finances, workforce roles, equipment, certifications, past projects (typed in or extracted from company documents). Each fact is marked Verified, Estimated, Assumption, Missing, etc.
2. **Create a project** for a tender and upload its documents (tender, addenda, bill of quantities, specification, contract).
3. **Process documents**: text is extracted and sanitised; processing status is always visible and failures always carry a reason.
4. **Extract requirements and facts** with supporting quotations; the system checks each quotation against the source.
5. **Review**: a person confirms, edits or rejects extracted items; conflicts between documents (for example an addendum changing a deadline) are flagged.
6. **Match and analyse gaps**: each requirement is compared with company evidence (Met / Partially met / Not met / Unknown / Not applicable) with the evidence used and the evidence still missing.
7. **Assess feasibility**: costs, bid price, break-even, cash flow, workforce and equipment gaps.
8. **Assess risks and compare scenarios.**
9. **Generate the decision briefing** and export it; a person records the final decision (pursue / decline).

## 7. Non-functional requirements

- **Privacy**: tender and company documents are processed locally; no outbound calls are needed for core use (see [SECURITY_PRIVACY.md](SECURITY_PRIVACY.md)).
- **Traceability**: any displayed finding can be traced to a document, a location and a quotation.
- **Determinism**: financial results are reproducible from stored inputs.
- **Robustness**: hostile or corrupt files fail with a clear reason instead of crashing or hanging.
- **Testability**: every module has automated tests; extraction quality is measured against a demo answer key (see [PROMPTS.md](PROMPTS.md)).
- **Portability**: SQLite by default; the schema avoids vendor-specific features so another database can be adopted later.

## 8. Scope

The split below follows the dependency order of the system: nothing downstream is trustworthy until ingestion, evidence and verification exist. It is a proposal; adjust it if priorities differ.

### Phase 1: MVP ("evidence-grounded fit check")

A single contractor can load a company profile and a tender and get a verified-requirements list with gaps and a first briefing.

- Company profile and capability management (FR-1 to FR-4)
- Ingestion of PDF, DOCX, XLSX and text with visible processing status (FR-5 to FR-8)
- Evidence-grounded requirement and fact extraction with quote checking, schema validation and contradiction detection (FR-10 to FR-15)
- Company-tender matching and gap analysis (FR-16, FR-17)
- Human verification workflow (FR-18)
- A basic evidence-backed briefing (FR-19) using local Gemma via Ollama (FR-20)
- Minimal web UI for the above and one-command setup (FR-28 core, FR-29)

### Phase 2: Commercial decision support

- Financial feasibility, bid pricing, break-even and cash flow (FR-21, FR-22)
- Risk identification and assessment (FR-23)
- Scenario comparison (FR-24)
- Exports, Ask-AI panel and audit trail (FR-25 to FR-27)

### Later

- OCR for scanned documents (FR-9)
- Multi-user accounts and permissions (FR-30)
- Additional database back ends and migration tooling
- Portfolio view across many tenders; learning from won/lost bids
- Additional languages and tender formats

### Explicit non-goals

- Automatic bid/no-bid decisions
- Legal or financial advice
- Cloud-hosted multi-tenant SaaS (not in scope before "Later")
- Storing individual employee records (only role-level capacity is stored)

## 9. Success criteria

For the MVP, measured on the demo tender set and on real tenders with permission:

- Every displayed requirement has a quotation that is found in the source (quote-verification rate of 100% for items shown as extracted; failures are flagged, never hidden).
- Extraction recall and precision against the answer key are tracked per prompt version.
- No requirement is shown as *Met* without recorded evidence.
- A first-time user can go from upload to a reviewed requirements list without developer help.
